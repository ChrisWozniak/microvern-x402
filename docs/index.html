Designed for careful decisions

Review, then decide in your own wallet.

1. Validate for free

MicroVern confirms that the request is bounded and unsigned before a quote is involved.

2. See exact terms

The quote identifies network, USDC asset, receiver, and price. A quote is not a payment.

3. Inspect before signing

A compatible x402 wallet or agent receives the report. You retain control over whether to sign the unchanged group.

Important limitations

MicroVern is deterministic decision support, not a guarantee of safety or financial advice. It does not replace a wallet’s consent screen. Independently verify recipients, amounts, and the transaction group before signing.
